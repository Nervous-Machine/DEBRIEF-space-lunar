# Asks — Lunar radiation campaign (lunar orbit), built on the Mars structure

Each open gap below is a place a contribution can land. A contribution is one cycle in this repository's ledger, under your name. How to contribute, and the templates: https://github.com/Nervous-Machine/DEBRIEF/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md

## 1. Nothing is measured at the lunar surface (ledger id G2)

Everything in this record is from lunar orbit. The one long-running surface measurement exists, and its data is not yet in this record.

Status: immovable.

- **the Chang'e-4 lander's dosimeter series from the far side, as a data cycle under the instrument team's name and terms** — contribution kind: data; who could act: the instrument team that holds the data.
- **a dosimeter at the surface, read hourly** — contribution kind: data; who could act: the first rover or lander team.

Signal expected: an hourly surface dose series over at least one solar rotation, from the Chang'e-4 lander's dosimeter or a new lander. Resolution by: none registered.

## 2. Earth-side alerts miss events at the Moon (ledger id G3)

Three episodes in the first half of 2024 were missing from the official catalog. Anyone operating near the Moon needs lunar-vicinity monitoring.

Status: in cycle.

- **a dose monitor in the lunar vicinity whose alerts do not wait on Earth's catalog** — contribution kind: data; who could act: an operator with a lunar-orbit or surface asset.

Signal expected: a lunar-vicinity dose monitor whose alert times can be set against Earth's catalog over one season. Resolution by: none registered.

## 3. The magnetotail effect runs opposite to the textbook (ledger id G1)

The magnetotail's effect on dose behaves opposite to the textbook expectation. Seventeen years of lunar-orbit data hold only forty-six days that were both in the magnetotail and stormy.

Status: in cycle.

- **more days that are both in the magnetotail and stormy, which in practice means a surface or far-side stream** — contribution kind: data; who could act: a lunar surface or far-side instrument team.

Signal expected: enough days that are both inside the magnetotail and geomagnetically stormy, from a surface or far-side instrument, to re-test the sign. Resolution by: none registered.

## 4. Crew transit dose to Mars has never been measured continuously (ledger id G4)

Background, not a result of this campaign: on the way to Mars there are four one-way snapshots in twenty-five years.

Status: immovable.

- **a small hosted dosimeter on any Mars-bound flight, read hourly** — contribution kind: data; who could act: a rideshare provider.

Signal expected: a continuous hourly dose series from a spacecraft on a Mars-bound trajectory. Resolution by: none registered.

## 5. Do solar-wind structures seen at Mars reach the Moon? (ledger id G8)

Stream interfaces corotate with the Sun and last weeks, so a structure that hit Mars should meet Earth and the Moon some days earlier or later. One pre-registered test found June suggestive and February absent. The test needs repeating with a particle flag that catches decay tails.

Status: in cycle.

- **a second cycle (c2) under the amended particle flag written in the c1 report, on the public CRaTER and OMNI data, pre-registered first** — contribution kind: rerun; who could act: anyone; the data is public at PDS and SPDF and the rule is written.

Signal expected: a c2 whose both base rates and the M3 statistic are printed; agreement with c1 at M3 under a clean baseline, or not. Resolution by: none registered.
